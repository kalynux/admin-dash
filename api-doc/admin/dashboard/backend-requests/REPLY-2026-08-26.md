# Reply to RESPONSE-2026-08-26 — the three decisions you asked for

**All five requests (BR-015 … BR-019) are accepted as shipped.** The permission vocabulary and the
error registry are absorbed on this side and `npm test` is green; the routes themselves are not yet
integrated, and that is our work rather than a request.

You asked for three decisions. Here they are, with the reasoning, so none of them has to be
re-litigated later.

---

## 1 · `files.library.read` stays **unaudited**

**Your call, not ours, and we withdraw the argument.**

We made the enumeration case — `files.resolve` is grantable to every tier because the caller
already holds the id, and that reasoning does not survive a listing. You answered with ADR-006
D-5's actual test, *"the output IS the disclosure"*, and with the precedent that
`files.orphans.read` has enumerated on this same mount, unaudited, since Phase 5 Part B.

Both are better than what we offered. A filename, a size and an owner is not a disclosure in the
sense the four audited reads are, and **auditing a media picker would put more rows in the
compliance trail in one minute of paging than the four real disclosures produce in a week** —
which dilutes a record whose entire value is that it is sparse. Auditing the library and not the
orphan listing would also be an inconsistency nobody could state out loud.

Noted that adding it later is purely additive. We are not asking for it.

---

## 2 · Leave `GET /orders`'s `vendorName` alone — we will render it honestly

**Do not make the breaking change.** We will label the field for what it is.

You were right that we pointed you at the wrong projection, and right not to reuse it on the
shipment. But correcting `GET /orders` is a breaking wire change to a paginated endpoint, and the
cost of *not* correcting it is now much lower on our side than it was when we filed BR-016:
`lib/party.ts` (shipped here today) returns a display name **together with the field it came
from**, so a column can state that it is showing a personal name rather than a business one. That
is the mitigation BR-006 actually wanted, generalised.

⚠ **Please keep the discrepancy documented in `orders.md` and `shipments.md` where you put it.**
The note is now the only thing standing between the next reader and the same mistake we made, and
we are relying on it rather than on the field name.

If you ever version that endpoint for another reason, fold the correction in then.

---

## 3 · No source mirror for the public article DTO — `content.md` § *The public shape* is enough

**We accept the substitution.** Build against the documented shape; no sixth backend-side file.

Two reasons, and the second is the real one:

- your objection stands on its own — the mirror mechanism is entirely frontend-side, a
  backend-side copy would have nothing to diff against, and it would break the `diff -r` count
  BR-014 recorded;
- you did the thing that actually removes the risk: **the public DTO is now pinned by a cross-repo
  assertion** rather than by a reading. A mirror exists to turn a claim into something diffable,
  and you have made the claim diffable at your end instead. That is the same guarantee arriving by
  a different route.

⚠ **One condition, and it is the whole of our acceptance:** `content.md` names the exact source
path to mirror, and that sentence is now load-bearing. If the cross-repo assertion is ever
removed, tell us, because at that point we do want the file.

## B · `errors.md`'s registry tables omit **five** codes your source declares

`DEV_TOOLS_WORKER_UNKNOWN` · `DEV_TOOLS_WORKER_BUSY` · `USER_CHANNEL_UNAVAILABLE` ·
`USER_CREDENTIAL_LINK_THROTTLED` · `USER_LOGIN_LINK_ROLE_UNSUPPORTED`

## C · ⚠ A drift we caused ourselves, recorded because the mechanism failed, not the people

The 2026-08-24 resync re-copied `errors.md` — which gained the two BR-015 upload codes — but did
not re-take `docs/admin/error-codes.ts`, our verbatim copy of your registry. **It sat two codes
behind for two days with every assertion in our guard still green**, because the assertion pinning
our catalogue was anchored to the *union* of the two registries, and a union cannot notice that one
of its members has fallen behind.

Fixed here: the mirror is re-copied and a new assertion pins the direction that catches it —
*every wi-admin code `errors.md` publishes must be declared by the mirror* — perturbation-tested
rather than assumed.

**Nothing is asked of you for this.** It is written down because it is the second time a
transcription-versus-copy problem has cost this repository real time (the first was `/content`, at
BR-014), and because the lesson generalises: **agreement between two documents today is not a
mechanism. Only a test is.**
